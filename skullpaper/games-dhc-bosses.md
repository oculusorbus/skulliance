# DHC Bosses

Three bosses stand up every **Thursday at 4pm CT** with a health bar each, and **everybody
shares them**. You can send **three Crews a day**; every point of damage anyone
lands comes off the same three bars, all week.

It has **its own page** at DHC Bosses in the games menu, with its own Crew picker and
its own ladder. It is the same engine as [[games-dhc-arena]] underneath, which is why the
board, the gems, the reach rule and the bombs are identical, but the two are separate
games in every way that affects you: a separate daily allowance, a separate ladder, and
most importantly **a separate recovery clock**. A Fighter resting from a ranked Arena
battle can still be sent at the bosses, and the other way round.

## The crew

| | |
|---|---|
| **D34THBRNGR** | Three bodies, one wall. The most health, the slowest hands. |
| **CIP-666** | The bruiser. Hits back hardest of the three. |
| **X-K4T** | Fast and vicious. Falls soonest, hurts most getting there. |

They are the same Fighters you fight in the Arena, with much larger numbers.
Same board, same matching, same reach, same bombs. What is different is that
there are three of them at once and their health does not reset when your run
ends.

## The cache: one trait for every day you turn up

Every day you send a Crew that does real damage banks **one trait**, up to
seven. Miss a day and you finish the week with six.

Three Crews a day is deliberately too few to bank the week in one sitting, so
a full cache is seven separate visits. You can see it filling on the Bosses
page: seven boxes, numbered 1 to 7, with the current one outlined and any you
missed dotted out.

The boxes are **numbered rather than named** because the week turns at 4pm, so
a day here runs 4pm to 4pm and straddles two dates. "Day 3" is true wherever
you are; "Saturday" would not be.

The whole cache rolls when the week closes, on the table the community earned.

## Losing is the normal outcome, and it still pays

A boss has far more health than one Crew can remove, so most runs end with
your Fighters down and the bar a little lower. **That is the feature working.**
The day's reward is decided by damage, not by kills, so a run that got nowhere
near a kill still counts for exactly what it dealt.

## What the day pays

Which table your cache rolls on depends on how far **the whole community**
pushed the crew over the week:

| Crew damage | Table |
|---|---|
| under 25% | standard |
| 25% or more | improved |
| 50% or more | rare |
| **all three down** | **best** |

Every table keeps a chance of every rarity, including mythic. Nothing is
guaranteed; what improves is the odds.

The best table is **not** the top of that gradient. At 99% with one boss on a
sliver you are still one table short, which is the whole point of the last
push.

Because the reward rises continuously with damage, there is never a moment
when stopping is worth more than carrying on.

## Your Fighters rest separately

A Fighter that fights a boss is unavailable for **bosses** for a few hours,
and longer if it fell. It is not benched from the Arena, and an Arena Crew
is not benched from the bosses. The two rosters are the same Fighters with
two independent cooldowns, so taking on the boss crew never costs you a
ranked battle.

## How big they are

The crew is sized every Thursday from how much firepower the platform could
actually field over a week: how many players have been active, how many Crews
they could send between them, and how much damage a run is worth. It is locked
when the crew stands up, so nobody who joins later makes it harder for the
people already fighting.

A quiet week gets a smaller crew. A busy one gets a bigger crew and the same
chance of clearing it. It is never sized beyond what the week can physically
deliver, so a perfect week always clears it.

## The weekly ladder

Ranked on **damage per Fighter deployed**, not on total damage. Sending more
Fighters is not the same as fighting well, and a player with five good
Fighters should be able to top it. Total damage, Crews sent and Fighters sent
are all shown; none of them decide the order.

CARBON is paid down the board by rank when the week closes on Thursday, the same harmonic
split every board uses.

There is **no all-time board** here, and that is deliberate: the ranking is damage per
Fighter within one week, and adding that up across every week ever would put a player who
sent one perfect Crew in March above somebody who has turned up every day since. The week
is the season.

## If nobody finishes them

A new crew stands up on Thursday at 4pm at full health. Nothing carries over, and
nothing is lost: everybody who took part is paid their cache either way, just
on a lower table.
