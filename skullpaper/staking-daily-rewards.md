# Daily Rewards

## Reward Rate

For up to 7 days, the reward increases for every day in a row that you claim your reward.

* Day 1: +100 RANDOM
* Day 2: +300 RANDOM
* Day 3: +500 RANDOM
* Day 4: +1,000 RANDOM
* Day 5: +1,500 RANDOM
* Day 6: +2,000 RANDOM
* Day 7: +3,000 RANDOM

## Daily Consumable

In addition to RANDOM points, each daily claim also awards one **mission consumable**, and which item you receive is tied to your current streak day:

* Day 1: Random Reward
* Day 2: 25% Success
* Day 3: Fast Forward
* Day 4: 50% Success
* Day 5: 75% Success
* Day 6: Double Rewards
* Day 7: 100% Success

So a full 7-day streak hands you the complete consumable set, including the two Legendary items (Double Rewards on day 6 and 100% Success on day 7). See [[missions-consumable-items]] for what each item does.

## A trait every day

Every claim draws a **DHC Fighters trait**, and the day of your streak decides which rarity table it comes from. Showing up is what pays; finishing the week is what pays best.

* Days 1-2: the ordinary drop table
* Days 3-4: the top-ten table
* Days 5-6: the top-three table
* Day 7: **the first-place table** - the best on the platform, and nothing else awards it for anything but winning outright

It used to be day seven only, off the top-three table, so six days of showing up bought nothing. A streak is a habit, and a habit wants paying as it forms rather than only when it is finished.

One per day. See [[games-dhc-fighters]] for what a trait is and what it is for.

## Streaks

A maximum of **8,400** random reward points can be claimed via a completed 7 day streak.

Once a streak of 7 days is completed or you miss a day on claiming your reward, your streak will reset and the daily random rewards will start over.

Total streaks completed are tallied on the daily rewards interface.

## Max Rewards

The ladder was raised 100x in November 2026. The old one paid 1 point on day one, which is not a reward, it is a rounding error - and points are no longer scarce enough to need that much caution.

The shape did not change: day seven still pays thirty times day one, so the streak is still the thing worth keeping.

**What it is calibrated against.** A perfect week pays 8,400 points, which buys 4,200 Realms location levels' worth at the any-points rate. A Level 10 Mine now produces 10,000 CARBON a night, which is 700 DIAMOND a week, which shatters into 4,200 core points. The two are deliberately equal: a flawless week of claiming is worth exactly the best Mine in the game, and that Mine still costs 5,500 points to build. See [[realms-locations]] and [[staking-crafting]].

**Upgrade costs did not move.** A location still costs `next level x 100`, so every point anybody had already banked buys exactly what it bought before. Raising what you earn is generosity; raising what things cost would have quietly devalued every balance on the platform.

## Crossover Rewards

Another benefit of this feature is that it allows non-members and base members the ability to collect points from projects they don't own yet and eventually experiment with crafting DIAMOND.

Even though you may earn points from a project you don't own yet, you will not show up on leaderboards until you actually own an NFT from that project.
