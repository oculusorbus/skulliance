# Drop Ship

## Overview

Drop Ship allows holders of the NFT collection to send their soldiers to battle through various scenes using a combination of armor, weapons, and items.

## Rewards

* **SCRIP** - in-game points rewarded to runner-ups that don't finish in the top 3 on the leaderboard
  * Amount is based on the participant's score
* **Theme currency** - Skulliance staking platform points awarded to the top 3 winners
  * 1,000 to 1st, 500 to 2nd, 250 to 3rd. The amounts are the same for every theme; only the currency changes.
  * Credited automatically the moment the week closes, on top of the in-game SCRIP economy.

| Theme | Pays |
| --- | --- |
| Drop Ship | DREAD |
| Dread City | DREAD |
| Filthy Mermaid | DREAD |
| Oculus Lounge | MOON |
| DHC2 Fighters | MAXI |

Each theme keeps its own board, its own squad and its own weekly cycle, so the
currency a player wins is the one belonging to the theme they played.

## Squads/Consumables

Send Drop Ship NFTs in squads of up to 4 into battle and browse consumables such as armor, weapons, and items that can be purchased to improve probabilities of getting higher scores. A squad with all armor, weapons, and item types is considered a Super Soldier Squad and has the best probability within the game.

**Armor sets the odds. Gear does not.** Only the armor your squad is wearing
decides the roll each round: the best tier worn sets the floor and every lower
tier adds a little on top, which is why a mixed squad of four beats four of the
same thing. The Squad panel shows the figure before you deploy, and it is the
same number the run rolls against.

**Every Medkit is one extra life, and they stack.** A Medkit carried by a
soldier and a Medkit bought from the store are two separate lives, and so are
two soldiers who both carry one. Each failed round spends exactly one of them
and the run continues. The first roll of a run happens on round 3, so that is
the single likeliest round to spend one on.

**Save a squad and field it again in one press.** Once soldiers are deployed,
**Save this squad** keeps that grouping. Saved squads sit above the barracks,
strongest first, each showing what it is: a full set of four armour tiers and
all three gear types is a **Super Soldier Squad**, and every squad shows what
its armour is worth a round. There are no names to invent.

**Deploy squad** fields all of them at once. **Disperse** breaks the grouping
up and returns the soldiers to the barracks so you can rebuild. While a
soldier belongs to a saved squad it is not deployed on its own, so a squad
cannot be taken apart by accident.

Your deployed squad lists the soldiers you added yourself first, in the order
you added them, and anything that arrived through **Deploy squad** after them
in armour order. **Withdraw all** clears the whole squad in one press, which
is what you want when you are setting several squads up back to back: build,
save, clear, build the next. It never touches a saved squad.

**A soldier belongs to one squad.** If somebody on the field is already in a
saved squad, saving says so and names them: disperse that squad first, or
withdraw them. Members are listed heaviest armour first, which is the order
the squad's odds are built in.

A squad only fields once per game, because soldiers who fight are out until
the next game opens. A squad resting between games says so rather than
looking broken. If you sell an NFT, the squad tells you which member is no
longer yours and offers to remove it, and a squad you have sold out of
entirely can still be seen and still be dispersed.

## The four free soldiers

Every Drop Ship player is given four Grunts - **Hagar, Delay, Blood Bag and
Sektr** - with no NFT required. Each carries a loaner kit, and the four of them
together cover every armor tier and three of the gear types, which is exactly a
**Super Soldier Squad**.

| Grunt | Armor | Gear |
|---|---|---|
| Hagar | Heavy | Melee |
| Delay | Medium | Demolition |
| Blood Bag | Light | Medkit |
| Sektr | Base | none |

**Deploy all four together.** Sent on their own they bring only their own kit,
so a single Grunt is much weaker than the squad. The loan is armor and gear
only: Grunts stay level 0 and keep their rank.

Grunts are a Drop Ship feature. Oculus Lounge has no equivalent.

## The other themes, and who can play them

The same game runs under several skins, each with its own collection, art,
wording and leaderboard: **Drop Ship**, **Dread City**, **Filthy Mermaid**,
**DHC2 Fighters** and [[games-oculus-lounge]]. Press **Select Game** on the dashboard and pick one by
its artwork. The one you are in is marked.

**One qualifying NFT is the gate.** Hold a single NFT from a theme's collection
and that theme is playable: the NFT becomes a soldier, and a deployed squad is
all the Play button asks for. Drop Ship is the one that needs nothing at all,
because of the four free Grunts above.

Filthy Mermaid used to say it needed five. It no longer does. The Shorty Verse
NFTs are expensive and getting hold of one is already the hard part.

Oculus Lounge is the exception, and not on holdings: it gates on a Discord role
that holding the VIP token does not grant by itself. See
[[games-oculus-lounge]].

If you have no soldiers for the theme you picked, the dashboard links straight
to that collection on Wayup, so you can see what is for sale before you commit
to anything.

## Trait drops

Taking the lead pays a **DHC Fighter trait**, and nothing else does.

If a run puts you top of the game currently running - or level with whoever is
top - and it also beats your own previous score in that game, it drops a
trait, rolled on the best odds table on the platform. Finishing second pays
nothing, and neither does a run that fails to improve on your own.

The first player into a fresh game clears both conditions by default. A new
game starts on a fixed cadence, so the lead comes back up for grabs every time
and no single score keeps it forever.

Bear in mind that your latest run replaces your previous one, so playing again
always risks the position you already hold. See [[games-dhc-fighters]].

Drops are announced in the DHC Fighters Discord channel, the same as every
other trait.

## Results in Discord

Every run posts to Discord on its own - how the run ended, and the standings
afterwards. This used to wait for you to sit through the whole replay and
press a button, so runs went unreported whenever somebody closed the tab. The
button at the end of the replay now just refreshes the page.

Both posts go out for **every theme**, each to its own channel and with its own
artwork. Dread City and Filthy Mermaid used to announce the death and not the
standings.

Play Drop Ship from the site nav's Play menu, or head straight to [dropship/dashboard.php](dropship/dashboard.php) - signing in to Skulliance is required now.
