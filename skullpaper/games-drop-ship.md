# Drop Ship

## Overview

Drop Ship allows holders of the NFT collection to send their soldiers to battle through various scenes using a combination of armor, weapons, and items.

## Rewards

* **SCRIP** - in-game points rewarded to runner-ups that don't finish in the top 3 on the leaderboard
  * Amount is based on the participant's score
* **DREAD** - Skulliance staking platform points for Ohh Meed awarded to the top 3 winners
  * 1K, 500, 250 DREAD

## Squads/Consumables

Send Drop Ship NFTs in squads of up to 4 into battle and browse consumables such as armor, weapons, and items that can be purchased to improve probabilities of getting higher scores. A squad with all armor, weapons, and item types is considered a Super Soldier Squad and has the best probability within the game.

## The four free soldiers

Every Drop Ship player is given four Grunts - **Hagar, Delay, Blood Bag and
Sektr** - with no NFT required. Each carries a loaner kit, and the four of them
together cover every armor tier and three of the gear types, which is exactly a
**Super Soldier Squad**.

| Grunt | Armor | Gear |
|---|---|---|
| Hagar | Heavy | Melee |
| Delay | Medium | Demolition |
| Blood Bag | Light | Medkit |
| Sektr | Base | none |

**Deploy all four together.** Sent on their own they bring only their own kit,
so a single Grunt is much weaker than the squad. The loan is armor and gear
only: Grunts stay level 0 and keep their rank.

Grunts are a Drop Ship feature. Oculus Lounge has no equivalent.

Visit the Barracks of Registered Soldiers who have connected their wallets to Drop Ship and participated in games.

## Trait drops

Taking the lead pays a **DHC Fighter trait**, and nothing else does.

If a run puts you top of the game currently running - or level with whoever is
top - and it also beats your own previous score in that game, it drops a
trait, rolled on the best odds table on the platform. Finishing second pays
nothing, and neither does a run that fails to improve on your own.

The first player into a fresh game clears both conditions by default. A new
game starts on a fixed cadence, so the lead comes back up for grabs every time
and no single score keeps it forever.

Bear in mind that your latest run replaces your previous one, so playing again
always risks the position you already hold. See [[games-dhc-fighters]].

Drops are announced in the DHC Fighters Discord channel, the same as every
other trait.

## Results in Discord

Every run posts to Discord on its own - how the run ended, and the standings
afterwards. This used to wait for you to sit through the whole replay and
press a button, so runs went unreported whenever somebody closed the tab. The
button at the end of the replay now just refreshes the page.
