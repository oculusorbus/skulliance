# Activity

**Activity** is the platform's own feed of what is happening across Skulliance - the same announcements Skull Bot posts to Discord, mirrored onto a wall of cards you can browse without leaving the site.

It sits next to the Launchpad in the menu.

## What appears

Every announcement the platform posts to Discord: missions completed, Arena battles, daily rewards, Realm sieges, Crypt Crawl runs, Gauntlets, store purchases, auctions, raffles, trait drops, leaderboard results. A new game or feature that announces anything shows up here the day it launches - nothing has to be wired up for it separately.

A card carries whatever the announcement carried: the title, the description, the art, and who it was about. Clicking it takes you to whatever the announcement linked to. Where an announcement mentions a player, the card shows their Skulliance name.

## What does not appear

* **Anything that was never announced.** Activity records announcements as they are sent, so a result that Skull Bot does not post is not here either.
* **Posts Discord rejected.** A card is written only once Discord has accepted the post, so the feed never claims something was announced when it was not.
* **System and error reports.** Operational alerts to the team are not player activity and are kept out.

## Filters

* **All activity** - the whole platform.
* **My activity** - things you did.
* **Involving me** - things other people did *to* you. A Gauntlet run against one of your NFTs, an Arena battle fought while you were away, a live match someone challenged you to, somebody winning an auction or raffle you listed.

The last two do not overlap: anything you started is under *My activity* and never repeated under *Involving me*, so the second tab is only the things you would otherwise never have known about. An empty *Involving me* just means nobody has come at you yet.
* **Channel** - narrow to one source, for example only [[games-dhc-arena]] or only [[missions]]. Only channels that actually have activity are listed.

## History

The feed starts from the day it went live. Announcements are recorded as they are sent, and much of what Skull Bot says - a monthly standing, a raid summary, a trait roll written out in words - exists only as that sentence, so there is nothing to backfill from.

Activity is kept for **120 days** and then falls off the wall. The feature it came from is still the record of it: your missions, battles, and rewards all live in their own pages.

## Visibility

Activity cards show the same names Skull Bot already announced in Discord. They are not currently filtered by the visibility setting described in [[platform-dashboard]]; if you want your results kept off public pages entirely, that is worth raising.


## The daily digest

Activity also feeds a poster. In the admin area, **Daily Digest** turns one day of the platform into a single image: the art that actually appeared that day, a tally of what happened, and the players who turned up, with their avatars.

The arrangement is seeded by the date, so the same day always draws the same poster and no two days look alike - the shape of it is dictated by what people did. Pick a day, pick a shape (wide, tall or square), and download the PNG.

It can only show what was announced, for the same reason the feed can: see *History* above.

## Your own day

The first time you open Skulliance on a new day, you get a small poster of the day before: how much you got through, which features it was, the pictures your activity produced, and your login streak if you have one going.

You can **download** it, **share it on X**, or close it. It asks once a day and nothing happens if you say no.

Two things worth knowing:

* **A quiet day shows nothing at all.** If you did not do anything yesterday, there is no modal - a poster announcing that you did nothing is not worth drawing.
* **Sharing is one click and the picture comes with it.** X cannot attach a file to a post written for you, so the poster is saved on Skulliance first and the post links to it - X reads the image from there. Nothing to attach by hand.
* **A shared poster is public, and only if you share it.** Pressing Share publishes that one image at its own link; closing the modal or downloading it publishes nothing. Shared posters are kept for about four months and then removed.

It is built from the same record the feed is, so it shows what was announced - the same limit described under *History*.

## Inviting someone

The link your digest shares is also your invite. Anyone who signs in through it gets **3 DHC traits**, and so do you.

* It pays for people who are **new to Skulliance, or who have not been on in a month**. An active player following your link is just a visit - nothing is owed for that.
* The traits are drawn on the **same odds a seven-day streak pays**, not the ordinary drop table: 25% common instead of 55%, and a real chance at a legendary.
* **Once per person.** The same friend coming back next month does not pay again.
* Nothing is paid for posting. The traits land when somebody actually signs in.

Your three arrive as the usual trait reveal the next time you open the site, and the bot sends you a message when it happens so you know even if you are away.

If you would rather not post publicly, the modal has a ready-written Discord message with your link in it - for someone you actually know. A cold message to a stranger is what Discord suspends accounts over, and that is your account, not ours.
