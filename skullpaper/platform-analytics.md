# Analytics

Analytics is the platform-wide transparency dashboard - a public view of overall activity across all of Skulliance. It was built for transparency and is available for anyone to view, logged in or not.

## What It Shows

* **Community** - registered stakers, NFTs staked, wallets connected, active Realms, and supported collections.
* **Engagement** - daily reward claims, missions, and raids, both monthly and all-time.
* **Gaming** - plays across every game on the platform: [[games-skull-swap]], [[games-monstrocity]], [[games-boss-battles]], [[games-cryptcrawl]], [[games-cryptconquest]], Realm Guardians, [[games-skullracer]], [[games-obscura]], [[games-gauntlets]], [[games-dhc-fighters]] and [[games-dhc-arena]].
* **Specialty Games** - [[games-drop-ship]] and [[games-oculus-lounge]], which run on their own database and are scored by game round rather than by date.
* **Projects** - core and partner project staking totals, Diamond Skull delegations, and faction sizes.
* **Economy** - transactions, store claims, crafting, mission and raid rewards, and location upgrades.
* **Trends** - time-series charts filterable by week, month, year, all-time, or a custom range. The metric dropdown is grouped into Platform, Realms & Missions, and Games, and every game above is charted except Drop Ship and Oculus Lounge, whose runs are filed against a game round and carry no date to plot.

A game's numbers here count the same thing its [[platform-leaderboards]] Activity board counts: completed runs rather than started ones for the solo games, resolved encounters for Gauntlets, one row per fallen realm for Realm Guardians.

Analytics aggregates platform totals only - it is not a personal dashboard and shows no individual balances. For your own complete history, see your [[platform-transactions]] ledger.
