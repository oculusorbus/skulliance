# Store

The Store is where stakers redeem their points for exclusive incentives - NFTs and items contributed by Skulliance and its partner creators.

## Claiming

* Claiming items requires Skulliance **membership** (see [[staking-membership]]). You can stake and earn points without membership, but you must be a member to claim from the store.
* Items can be claimed with the matching **project points**, and **DIAMOND** can claim *any* item - at a **discount** for core offerings and a **premium** for partner offerings. See [[staking-points]].

## Browsing

* Items are filterable by **project**.
* A dedicated **Exclusive Items** filter highlights limited offerings.
* Each item opens a detail view with its full image.

## Submissions

Approved creators can submit items to the store through the submission form, making the store a continually refreshed pool of community rewards.

A submitted listing is not frozen. A creator can go back and correct their own listings - the ones credited to a project they run - changing the name, the image, the price, the quantity, and which of their projects it is credited to. So a typo, a dead image link or a price agreed after submission gets fixed in place rather than by relisting.

Setting the quantity to **0** takes an item off the shelf without deleting it; **-1** means unlimited.

Two things stay with the platform rather than the creator: whether a listing is flagged **Exclusive**, and whether it also accepts a **second project's currency**. Both spend something that is not the creator's to spend - a slot at the top of the store, or another project's points.
