# Testimonials

Players saying why they play, in their own words, shown to people who are
deciding whether to start.

## How you get asked

You are asked after you take a realm, not when you arrive. Somebody who has
just won a raid has an answer ready; somebody who just opened the page came to
play, not to write.

You are only asked if you have a realm and have won a raid in the last couple
of days. Signing up is not enough, because a testimonial from somebody who has
not played says nothing a stranger can use.

**You will be asked three times at most**, at least a fortnight apart, and the
last one says it is the last one. "Not now" is a fortnight. On the final ask it
is "No thanks", and that is the end of it. Writing one also stops the asking.

## What makes a good one

The modal asks you a question rather than handing you a blank box: what keeps
you coming back, what you would tell somebody deciding whether to start, or the
best thing that has happened to you here.

What helps is the specific thing a newcomer would not guess. What drew you in,
what you keep showing up for, what surprised you. The point is to help people
decide whether this is for them, and to grow the server for everybody already
in it.

Links are not carried, and there is a minimum length, because one line cannot
tell anybody anything.

## Who sees it

Nothing is published automatically. An owner reads every testimonial before it
appears anywhere.

When you write one you choose how it is attached to you:

- **With your name and avatar.** The default, and the most useful to a reader.
- **Without your name.** It shows as "A Skulliance player".
- **Off the site entirely.** Kept, never shown.

You can change that any time from your profile, including after it is
published, and it takes effect on the next page load. You do not need anybody's
permission to take your name off something you wrote.

**A Discord post cannot be unposted.** Once an approved testimonial has gone to
the testimonials channel, that message stays, even if you later set yours to
private. The site will stop showing it; Discord will not.

## Changing what it says

Editing the words writes a new testimonial and sends it back to be read again.
That is deliberate: an approval that could be edited afterwards would not be an
approval of anything.

Your previous versions are kept. Nothing you have written is deleted.

## Sharing it

After you send one you can post it to X in one tap. It is prefilled with your
words, a mention of the Skulliance account so it can be picked up, and a link
to the [[realms]] world map.
