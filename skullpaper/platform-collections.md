# Collections

The Collections page is the registry of every NFT collection Skulliance supports, on every chain it runs on - each identified by whatever that chain uses to name a collection.

## What It Shows

* Every supported collection and the **project** it belongs to.
* The **chain** each one is on, with its logo, so a list spanning several
  chains can be read at a glance rather than by recognising identifiers.
* Filtering by **project** and by **chain**. The two work together - picking a
  chain keeps the project you were looking at, and the other way round. The
  chain filter only appears when there is more than one chain to choose
  between.
* Each collection's reward rate, which drives nightly staking yield.
* A link out to each collection's marketplace, so a reward rate you like is
  one click from buying into it.

Collections are the link between an on-chain NFT and its in-platform identity: when you stake, the platform matches your NFT to a collection here to determine its project, rewards, and eligibility.

**What identifies a collection depends on the chain.** Cardano uses the policy id; the XRP Ledger uses the issuer and taxon; Solana uses the collection address; an EVM chain like Polygon uses the contract address. You never have to supply any of these - your wallet is read for you - but it is why the same collection on two chains is two rows here rather than one.

## Buying Into a Collection

Collection names on this page link straight to that collection on **Wayup**, built from its policy ID - so if you see a reward rate you like, you can go and buy into it without hunting the policy down yourself. Links open in a new tab. A collection whose policy is not on file simply shows as plain text rather than a broken link.
