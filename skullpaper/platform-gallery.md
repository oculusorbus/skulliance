# Gallery

The Gallery is a discovery wall of NFTs from across the Skulliance community, highlighting what's actively in play.

## What It Shows

* A rotating pool of NFTs from **public, active** stakers, plus your own full collection when you're logged in.
* Activity indicators layered onto each NFT: whether it's currently on a **mission or raid**, or **delegated to a Diamond Skull**.
* Only NFTs from stakers who've set their profile to visible appear publicly (see [[platform-dashboard]] for the visibility setting).

The Gallery is a good way to see which collections are active, spot Diamond Skull delegations, and browse the breadth of art across the ecosystem.

## Music Videos

Separately from the Gallery, **Music Videos** under the NFTs menu collects
music videos made using art from the projects and artists in Skulliance. It is
a **public page** - no login, because the point of a music video is being
watched - and it is ordered by project name.

Each one is a tile showing that project's **realm theme** artwork. Press it and
the video opens over the page at its own resolution - never blown up larger
than it was made - and shrunk to fit only if your window is too small for it.
Press Escape or click outside it to close. The
films are only downloaded when you open one, so the page itself is light.

Tiles are captioned with that project's Monstrocity theme logo where it has
one, and with its points icon and name where it does not. New videos appear as
soon as they are uploaded; nothing has to be added to a list.
