## What we're actually building, so you know exactly what you're agreeing to

Stakers earn individual trait pieces through activity on Skulliance — playing
games, staking — and then assemble them into characters they can use as avatars
and, later, in games on the platform.

The limits, plainly:

- **Nothing is minted.** No NFTs get created. Not now, not later.
- **Stakers don't own the traits.** This is a feature inside the platform, not a
  claim on your artwork.
- **Characters can't be sold, traded, or exported**, and they're never presented
  as collection items.
- **The real collection stays the real collection.** We'll link people to it, so
  anyone who finds your work this way can go and buy the actual thing.

The intent is to celebrate DHC2 and put it in front of more people — not to make
a free imitation of it. If any part of this sits wrong with you, say so and
Jeremiah and I will change it or drop it. You have the final say.

Thank you for trusting us with the source art. It's a remarkable collection and
I'm looking forward to seeing people discover it.

## What each one settles

**1 — the 37 on-chain traits with no art stay out.** Not an oversight: they are
1/1 pieces, painted directly into hand-assembled Fighters and never split into
layers. No action. They remain excluded from the collectible pool, and the
absence test never sees them, so they cannot be scored.

**2 — every pair I listed is two traits, not one spelling.** Checked each
against the art actually delivered:

| pair | resolution |
|---|---|
| ZX50 Centinel / ZX350 Sentinel | different torsos. Only ZX350 has art; ZX50 (2 fighters) is a 1/1, excluded |
| MK100 Blaster Arms / Damaged | different arms. **Both have art** — `Deteriorated_MK100_Blaster_Arms.png` is the damaged one, already aliased |
| XLONs 2YK Attack / #2 | different backgrounds. Files `#01`/`#02` cover two of the three on-chain names; the bare name (2 fighters) has no art, excluded |
| XLONs Complex / Complex Trap | different backgrounds. Trap (4 fighters) has no art, excluded |
| DHC2 Comic Cover #3 / #4 | different effects. #4 (3 fighters) has no art, excluded |

These are recorded as **DO NOT MERGE** in `gen-rarity.py`. They read like typos
and are not; merging any of them would fold a 1/1 piece into a common trait.

**3 — the draw order is confirmed.** Note the platform has since added
exceptions on top of it (effects under the head, Code Sea Predator behind arms,
Xlon's Black Fire and Perforator behind torso). Those are Oculus Orbus's calls
for specific pieces, not contradictions of this answer.

**4 — confirms the weapon front/back split we already build.** `dhcf_slots()`
is `background, weaponBack, torso, weapon, arms, effects, head, headgear,
companion` — the back half behind the torso, the front half over it, which is
what he describes. His "a belt or a strap usually" is the sash: exactly the
Scythe/Skull Krusher coupling already in `DHCF_COUPLED`.

One difference worth a look: he puts the front weapon **over the head**, twice
(both here and in the order he approved in 3). We draw it under. For a sash on
the chest the two are visually identical, which is presumably why it never
showed up — but if a front weapon ever rides high, his order is the correct one.

**5 — he agrees to the build as described**, including the pieces that never
made it into a minted Fighter. The rights framing in the brief he read is the
one on the live page: nothing minted, no ownership, no export, and a link back
to the real collection.
